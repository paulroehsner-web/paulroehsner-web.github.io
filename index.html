Paul-fitness-sync

Paul-fitness-sync is a private, single-user tool that Paul Roehsner uses to copy his own health and fitness data (activity, sleep, heart rate and body measurements) from the Google Health API into a personal database for his own tracking.

It is not offered to the public, and it only ever reads data from Paul's own Google account with his consent.
